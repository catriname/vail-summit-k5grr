VAIL SUMMIT

K5GRR Fork — Web Flasher

Flash VAIL SUMMIT firmware directly to your VAIL SUMMIT from the browser. No drivers, no Arduino IDE.

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop. This page must be served over HTTPS.
  1. Plug your VAIL SUMMIT into a USB port.
  2. Click Install VAIL SUMMIT above.
  3. Pick the device's serial port in the browser prompt.
  4. Wait for the flash to finish — the device will reboot automatically.
This is the K5GRR fork of VAIL SUMMIT. For the original project, see the upstream repository.
